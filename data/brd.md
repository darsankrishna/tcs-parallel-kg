# Business Requirements Document — Cash Transaction Report (CTR) Engine

Sample Bank Ltd · Version 1.0

> Illustrative BRD written for this prototype. Sample Bank, CoreOne, UCIC service, Treasury Rate Policy TRP-2023 and the CTR Working Group are fictional. The requirements deliberately mix knowledge from the regulation, from supervisory guidance, from the regulator's reporting format, and from the bank's own systems and decisions, the way real BRDs do.

## 1. Purpose

The CTR Engine prepares the monthly Cash Transaction Report that Sample Bank submits to FIU-IND under the PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005.

## 2. Business requirements

BR-01 Reporting scope. For each calendar month, the engine shall identify every cash transaction of a customer valued above INR 10,00,000, and every series of integrally connected cash transactions whose monthly aggregate exceeds INR 10,00,000, as required by Rule 3(1)(A) and Rule 3(1)(B).

BR-02 Cash identification. A transaction shall be treated as a cash transaction when its CoreOne transaction type is 'C' and its channel is Branch Teller or Cash Deposit Machine. Cash deposits through Cash Deposit Machines are in scope as agreed by the CTR Working Group on 12-Mar-2025. ATM cash withdrawals are out of scope.

BR-03 Integrally connected transactions. For each customer, identified by UCIC, all cash debits across all of the customer's accounts in the calendar month shall be summed separately from all cash credits. When either sum exceeds INR 10,00,000, the transactions on that side are reportable.

BR-04 Small-value exclusion. Within a reportable series, individual transactions below INR 50,000 shall not be listed in the report.

BR-05 Internal accounts. Transactions between the bank's own internal (office) accounts shall be excluded from the report.

BR-06 Foreign currency. Foreign-currency cash transactions shall be converted to INR at the Treasury card rate of the transaction date, as defined in Treasury Rate Policy TRP-2023 section 3, before any threshold is applied.

BR-07 Walk-in customers. Cash transactions by walk-in customers without an account shall be grouped using the PAN recorded on the transaction voucher.

BR-08 Report content. For each reported transaction the report shall contain the transaction date, amount, currency, nature of transaction (deposit or withdrawal), account number, customer name and branch code.

BR-09 Source mapping. Transaction attributes shall be sourced from CoreOne table TXN_HIST (TXN_DT, TXN_AMT, TXN_CCY, TXN_TYPE, CHNL_CD, ACCT_NO). Customer name shall be sourced from CUST_MASTER joined on UCIC.

BR-10 Report format. The report shall be produced in the FIU-IND account-based XML reporting format and shall pass the FIU-IND validation utility before submission.

BR-11 Timeline. The report for each month shall be submitted to FIU-IND by the 15th day of the succeeding month. A draft shall be generated by the 5th working day for review by the Principal Officer.

BR-12 Corrections. A report found to contain errors after submission shall be corrected by filing a replacement report.
