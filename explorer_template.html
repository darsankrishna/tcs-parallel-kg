CTR BRD Gap Map

A knowledge graph built from an excerpt of the Rules and a Business Requirements Document for a Cash Transaction Report engine. For every piece of knowledge in the BRD it asks: does the regulation say this? If not, what kind of knowledge filled the gap, and where inside the bank would it live?

Where the BRD's knowledge comes from

Three layers, one graph

The regulation contributes the normative layer, the BRD the specification layer. The provenance layer is the part neither document writes down: which clause justifies which piece of the BRD, and where it doesn't, a Gap node pointing at candidate sources.

Pipeline

  1. Parse the regulation into clauses and the BRD into requirements. Deterministic.
  2. Decompose each requirement into knowledge atoms with an exact quote as evidence (LLM, Prompt 1). The pipeline rejects any atom whose quote isn't found verbatim.
  3. Judge each atom against retrieved clauses: stated, refined (fills an open or delegated term), or none (LLM, Prompt 2).
  4. Analyse the regulation: defined terms, obligations, and delegation phrases by pattern; open terms proposed by the LLM and checked against the definitions.
  5. Query the graph for six gap kinds: unexplained knowledge, interpretation without authority, source named but absent, open term never filled, data never mapped, obligation with no requirement.
  6. Route each gap to candidate sources twice: what would justify it, and what would fulfil it. Score = type prior + text similarity + delegated authority + named-source match.
Regulation text: PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005 as published by FIU-IND. The BRD, Sample Bank, its systems, policies and internal sources are fictional and were written for this prototype. Atom extraction and judge labels were produced by Claude following the prompts in the prototype, not by an independent model run.
